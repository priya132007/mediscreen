# MediScreen AI design brief

## Theme Name
Clinical Signal / Human Review

## Intro
A calm, operational healthcare workspace that makes risk signals easy to scan while keeping clinician judgment visibly in control.

## Probability
0.08

## Design movement
Editorial clinical utility: information-dense enough for a high-volume hospital, softened by warm paper-like surfaces, quiet dividers, and generous rhythm.

## Core principles
1. Evidence before confidence.
2. Every risk state has a written reason.
3. Make the next action obvious for patients and staff.
4. Use color as a reinforcement, never as the only signal.

## Color philosophy
Warm off-white page background, deep navy ink, blue-teal brand accent, pale mint surfaces for confirmed information, amber for review-needed states, and controlled red only for potential red flags. Avoid gradients and decorative color noise.

## Layout paradigm
A responsive dashboard shell with a compact left rail on desktop, a simple top bar on smaller screens, strong page titles, and modular rounded cards. The patient flow uses a single main action per step. The doctor workspace uses a two-column scan pattern: patient context on the left, decision support on the right.

## Signature elements
- A flat “screen + pulse” mark with a teal signal line crossing a navy rounded rectangle.
- Thin vertical signal rail on priority cards.
- Evidence chips with short labels and source/date metadata.
- Timeline with a teal path and date nodes.

## Interaction philosophy
Progressive disclosure, inline corrections, optimistic edits, readable labels, and no hidden meaning behind hover. The patient experience uses large targets and plain-language prompts; the doctor view favors quick scan, edit, accept/reject, and feedback actions.

## Animation
Use restrained 150–220ms opacity/translate transitions for panel changes and progress states only. Avoid pulsing, flashing, or motion that could imply urgency beyond the written status.

## Typography system
Use a clean system sans stack with heavy navy display headings, compact uppercase metadata labels, 14–16px body text, and 12px evidence labels. Keep line lengths comfortable and never use all-caps for patient-facing instructions.

## Brand essence
“Prepared context, human judgment.”

## Brand voice
Calm, direct, respectful, and transparent: “Potential red flag — clinician review required” instead of diagnostic language.

## Wordmark/logo
MediScreen AI wordmark paired with a flat screen-and-pulse symbol. The symbol must remain legible at favicon size and use only broad filled shapes with clear negative space.

## Signature brand color
Signal teal: #138f8b, paired with deep navy #15324b.

## Asset decision
This is an operational dashboard and does not need hero photography or editorial imagery. Create one project-specific flat logo mark and reuse it in the header and favicon; do not add generic stock imagery.
